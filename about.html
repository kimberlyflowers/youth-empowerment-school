Raising children who say YES to a life of meaning.

We're a small, faith-rooted academy in the heart of Texas — built on the conviction that every child has a calling, and every calling deserves a school that takes it seriously.

Our mission

To empower, equip, and expand the next generation of leaders.

Youth Empowerment School exists to raise students who lead with conviction, serve with humility, and live with purpose — preparing them not just for college, but for a life that counts.

We believe a child's education should never be separated from their character. So we built a school where the math test, the chapel service, the soccer practice, and the mission trip are all part of the same formation — one continuous training for the kind of adult the world genuinely needs.

That faith is the foundation, not the extracurricular. That children rise to the standard you set for them. That servant-leadership is taught by example, not by lecture. And that small classes, known names, and prayed-over students change everything.

Our story

From a borrowed classroom to a graduating class.

YES began with twelve students, two teachers, and a borrowed classroom in a church basement. It began because a group of parents and pastors looked at the world their kids were inheriting and decided to build something better.

Eight years later, we've grown into a full K–12 academy. The classrooms are no longer borrowed. The student body has grown twentyfold. But the conviction is the same: every student is known, every day points toward something larger, and every graduate walks out ready.

2018
First classroom opens
Twelve students, two teachers, one conviction. YES begins as a K–6 program in a church basement.
2020
Middle school launches
Original families ask us to grow with their children. We add 7th–8th grade and our first dedicated campus.
2022
Upper School begins
9th grade opens. Students begin mission internships and capstone projects in their junior year.
2025
First graduating class
Nineteen seniors walk the stage. 100% accepted to college, ministry training, or vocational programs of their choice.
2026
Now enrolling
250+ students. Expanded campus. New scholarship fund opens doors for families who couldn't otherwise afford a YES education.
