About this event

Spend a Saturday morning with us. Tour every grade level, hear from current parents, sit in on student-led demos, and meet our admissions team one-on-one. Coffee & pastries on the house.

Designed for families considering YES for fall enrollment — but anyone curious about a faith-rooted, classically-grounded education is welcome. Bring your kids; we'll have student ambassadors leading age-appropriate activities while you tour.

Frequently asked

Should I bring my children?

Yes — please do! Student ambassadors lead age-appropriate activities throughout the morning so parents can tour and ask questions freely.

How long should I plan to stay?

The full program runs 9 AM to noon, with optional one-on-one time with admissions afterward. Most families stay the whole morning.

Can I apply at the event?

Absolutely. Our admissions team will have laptops and printed applications on hand, and we can walk you through the process right there.

What if I can't make this date?

We host monthly tours and are happy to schedule a private one. Email admissions@yesacademy.org and we'll find a time that works.
