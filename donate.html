Sponsor a seat. Shape a life.

Every gift to YES goes directly to our scholarship fund — opening doors for families who believe in our mission but can't carry the full cost on their own.

Where your gift goes

100% of donations fund tuition assistance.

YES is a non-profit ministry. Operating costs are covered by tuition. Donations exist for one purpose: to make sure no family who's called to YES is turned away because of money.

$50

Funds a week of classroom supplies for one student.

$250

Sponsors a service-trip seat for a middle schooler.

$1,000

Covers one quarter of partial scholarship aid.

$5,000

Funds a full-year seat for a student in financial need.

YES is a registered 501(c)(3). All donations are tax-deductible. EIN available on request.
